# App Store intelligence tools

> Apple publishes its App Store top charts (Top Free, Top Paid and Top Grossing, per country) through a legacy iTunes RSS feed that allows browser requests. The tool in this category reads that feed live, so the ranking, app name, developer and category you see are the real current chart, not a sample. Apple caps each chart at the top 100. A single snapshot answers where an app stands today; tracking rank movement over time across many countries is what the backing Apify actor is for.
