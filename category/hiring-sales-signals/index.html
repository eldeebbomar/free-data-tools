Hiring & sales signal tools

A company's open roles are one of the most honest signals it publishes: every requisition is budget someone approved. The tool in this category reads a company's live open roles straight from the public Greenhouse Job Board API in your browser, with no login or key, and buckets them into a signal such as a sales expansion, an engineering build-out or a data and AI push. When one company at a time is not enough, the same configuration hands off to an Apify actor that sweeps several applicant tracking systems at scale.
