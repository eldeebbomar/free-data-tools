# SaaS pricing intelligence tools

> Competitor pricing pages change quietly, and a missed change can cost a deal. The tool in this category is a query builder for tracking them: you list the pricing-page URLs to watch, and it assembles a ready-to-run configuration and previews a fixed example of the structured output, with plan names, monthly and annual prices, features and limits. It does not scrape pricing pages from your browser. The runs happen on the backing Apify actor, which you can schedule to detect plan and price changes over time.
