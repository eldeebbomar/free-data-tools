Real estate listing data tools

These tools cover property listings in two markets: Egypt (Aqarmap, Dubizzle Egypt and Property Finder Egypt) and Saudi Arabia (portals such as Bayut.sa, Wasalt, Aqar.fm and PropertyFinder.sa). Both are query builders. Each assembles a ready-to-run configuration and shows a fixed example of the listing record you will get, such as price, area, location, bedrooms and agent details, and for Saudi listings the REGA advertising-license number. Neither shows live listings in the browser; the export runs on the matching Apify actor as JSON or CSV.
